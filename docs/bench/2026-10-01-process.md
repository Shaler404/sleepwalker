# Process benchmark, 2026-10-01: the dream's process improver

The dream's main job is section 7 of `runbooks/dream.md`: find what repeats in the sessions and what it costs,
and propose fixes (runbook edits and tool proposals). Five candidates (model × effort) got the same job on the
same material: 36 sessions of 5 games from the first night, with inbox blocks, stats, playbooks and
`raw/*/steps.jsonl`. Each worked in its own worktree with a copy of the state, through `claude -p` (CLI 2.1.286).
Each wrote its edits and a pull request description, with nothing committed.

| Variant | Model run | Cost | Minutes | Changes | Stale local solver | Content-filter crashes | Lost videos | Restart reverts a won level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **fable:high** | claude-fable-5-1 | **$7.99** | 14.4 | 9 | **found, checked on frames** | crash loop | found | found |
| fable:medium | claude-fable-5-1 | $6.25 | 10.9 | 7 | — | crash loop | found | found |
| opus:high | claude-opus-5-5 | $4.75 | 12.7 | 6 | — | **cause: quoted text** | mentioned | found |
| opus:medium | claude-opus-5-5 | $2.63 | 8.6 | 8 | — | crash loop | mentioned | found |
| sonnet:high | claude-sonnet-5-5 | $2.97 | 9.3 | 15 | found | crash loop; quote cause rejected | found | found |

What the columns mean:
- **Stale local solver**: `solver_path()` preferred the local draft forever, so a solver fix merged by the
  dream never ran on the phone. Meowdoku placed 13 levels of cats by hand at 60–100 s each.
- **Content-filter crashes**: three Cryptogram sessions in a row died with "output blocked by content
  filter". That is 52 minutes of phone time with nothing saved.
- **Lost videos**: most originals are deleted before they are uploaded (the YouTube limit).
- **Restart reverts a won level**: Pull the Pin.

Every candidate found the problems everyone could see: the ad exits, wins recorded without a win frame,
`same_as_prev` crying wolf, crashed sessions that leave no trace. The candidates differ in what only one or two
of them found and in how they checked it:
- fable:high checked the solver claim by running both files on the recorded frame;
- opus:high traced the crashes to the frames they died on;
- sonnet:high was the broadest but edits the most text (108 lines in 6 files for one day).

## Choice

`process: fable:high`. It runs once a day, so the difference with the cheaper variants is about $5 a day. It
found the one bug that silently undid merged fixes, and it verified it. Sonnet:high is the cheaper alternative
if the cost matters, and a second benchmark is worth running once the post-session reviews (`reviews.md`) and
documenter logs exist: they are the process improver's main input and did not exist yet.

The findings themselves:
- the stale solver, the crash loop and the quoted-text rule were fixed at once (harness PR);
- the rest is in the process pull request built from the fable:high output.
