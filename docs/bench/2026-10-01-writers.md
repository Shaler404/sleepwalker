# Writers benchmark, 2026-10-01: the documenter

The documenter writes a feature page after every session, so it runs about as often as the player. The
benchmark gives every candidate (model × effort) the same job: rebuild an old-layout page in the new
layout (`runbooks/document.md`, "Rebuilding a page in the old layout"). Each candidate gets its own worktree
with a copy of the game's state, through `claude -p` (CLI 2.1.286; the models it ran are in the table).

Pages:
- **Pull the Pin · Collections**: four tabs, two sessions, the entry button on the level map;
- **Cryptogram · Quote Race**: an event with a launch popup, a tutorial, rules, a chest and a strip on
  the win screen; five sessions.

Frames are the images on the page and sources are the footnotes. Every page passed `check-pages`: the
check counts sections, not facts, so the pages were also read and their entry frames opened (the button
is circled on every one from opus:medium up).

## Choice

`documenter: opus:medium`. It is the cheapest variant that writes like the reference: every place has a
frame, the tabs have their own sections, it has as many sourced facts, and it is three times cheaper than
fable and 30 % cheaper than opus:high. Sonnet is cheaper still but drops facts. Haiku and opus:low skip the
frames the owner asked for.
