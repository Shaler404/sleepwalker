# Play benchmark, 2026-10-01: the model that plays mastered gameplay

The `play` role plays levels of a mechanic that is already learned, by the playbook or the mechanic's solver.
Each variant played 2 interleaved, rotated slots of up to 4 levels per game on the same phone (Samsung
SM-A276B), through `claude -p` (CLI 2.1.286). Bench slots never hand off and never change a mechanic's
status. Levels come in the game's order, so every variant meets the same stretch of the game.

"Won/h" counts levels won per hour of slot time, ads included. "Level" is the median time of a won level, and
"$/won" is the CLI's cost plus consultations (`ask`) per level won.

## Pull the Pin — manual (physics puzzles, read by eye)

| Variant | Won | Lost | Won/h | Level | $/won |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **opus:low** | **7** | **1** | **12.7** | 47 s | **$0.73** |
| sonnet:low | 5 | 6 | 9.1 | 35 s | $1.18 |
| sonnet:medium | 2 | 2 | 3.5 | 188 s | $2.81 |

**Choice: opus:low.** It loses one level in eight where sonnet loses about half. Losses cost replays and
ads, so it wins more levels an hour and is cheaper per level won.

## MeowTrail (Akari) — solver that reads the board from the frame (lab, 2026-10-01)

| Variant | Won | Lost | Won/h | Level | $/won |

| **sonnet:low** | **8** | 0 | **36.4** | 28 s | **$0.18** |

## Cryptogram — not measured

- **Slot 1 (sonnet:low)** spent its 19 minutes in ads on CONTINUE and never reached a board.
- **Slot 2 (sonnet:medium)** died with "Output blocked by content filtering policy" right after the level 16
  board came up, before typing anything. It is the fourth session to die there. The model recognizes the
  encoded text while reasoning, so the rule against writing quotes out is not enough.

The game is off in `games.yaml` until level 16 is passed by hand.

## Defaults

Effort `medium` never beat `low` on play: more thinking makes slower moves on gameplay that is already
learned. The project default becomes `play: sonnet:low`. That applies to Vita Mahjong, whose new solver
(lab, 2026-10-01) reads the board like the two solver games, and to every game without its own `models`.
Pull the Pin, where the player reads physics by eye, gets `opus:low`.

Not measured, on the owner's decision: sonnet:high, opus:medium and fable on the manual games, and the
`study` role. The study role learns a mechanic once, so it can be compared only when a new game is onboarded.
