# Skins (Default Skin button)

A grey "Default Skin" button at the bottom of Settings. A tap does nothing, on a fresh install and again
after Adventure level 5; no skin list, other skin, price or progress was seen [^s2] [^s3] [^s4].

## Why it appeared

In Settings from the first launch: a grey Default Skin button under Replay [^s1]. Hypothesis: other skins,
and an active button, come with progress or an event; not verified (still inactive after Adventure level 5
[^s4]).

## Where to find it

Classic board > the gear top right > Default Skin, the last button, grey with a T-shirt icon [^s2]
[^s5].

![The Settings popup on 6 October; the grey Default Skin button with a T-shirt icon is the last row, under Replay](../img/20261006-skins-entry-c1383ec7.webp) [^s5]
*The grey Default Skin button, last in Settings, under Replay*

## What it looks like

![Settings reopened after the tap on Default Skin: the same popup, the button still grey](../img/20261006-skins-screen-c1383ec7.webp) [^s6]
*After a tap on Default Skin: no skin screen, Settings stays as it was*

No screen of its own: tapping the button leaves Settings unchanged [^s3] [^s4].
The grey colour, unlike the green buttons above it, is the only sign that it is inactive; there is no lock,
price, bar or counter on or near it [^s2] [^s6].

## How it works

Version 10.8.1. One skin exists on screen, Default, and it is the one in use: the board, the blocks and the
tray keep one look in every session [^s1] [^s6]. No way to earn, buy or equip
another skin is shown [^s4].
