# Block Blast!

[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.block.juggle) · version 10.8.1

A block puzzle: drag tray pieces onto an 8x8 board, full rows and columns clear, the game ends when no piece fits. The app opens on an endless classic board; Back opens a home menu with a 96-level Adventure map (score and gem goals), Medal, a daily-victories counter, 8 built-in mini-games and cross-promo icons. Interstitials at game over and on Replay; no shop or revive seen yet.
