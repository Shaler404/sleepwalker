# Block Blast!

[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.block.juggle) · analysed on 10.6.5; Google Play has 10.8.0

An endless block puzzle: drag pieces from a tray of three onto an 8x8 board; full rows and columns
clear and score. There is no main menu: the game opens straight onto the classic board after a
one-move tutorial on the first launch, and the gear is the only entry point (Settings with Sound, BGM,
Vibration, More Games with 8 built-in mini-games, More Settings, Replay and an inactive skin button).
Interstitials show after Replay; none appeared during play.

![A new classic board: score 0, best score 261 by the crown, three pieces in the tray](img/20261001-classic-screen-be69c090.webp)
