# Cryptogram: Word Logic Puzzles

[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.crypt.gram.puzz) · version 3.6.1

A cryptogram word puzzle: each number stands for a letter of a quote. Lives (5), a 3-mistake counter and a hint bulb from level 2, Daily Tasks (unlock at 3 levels) and a Daily Challenge (15 levels), a banner ad from level 2.
