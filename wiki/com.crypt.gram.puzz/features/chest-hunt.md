# Chest Hunt event

A timed event: some cells of the regular levels carry a key, and placing the correct letter in such a cell collects it. Keys collected in a level are claimed on its win card, and 120 keys open a reward chest before the timer (2d 23h at the start) runs out [^s1] [^s2] [^s3] [^s4].

## Why it appeared

Level 6 was won and NEXT tapped; an ad that would not close was ended by restarting the game, and on the relaunch home opened with the "CHEST HUNT" popup: "The event has started!" and a 2d 23h timer [^s1]. Hypothesis: the event unlocks after 6 levels won and shows on the next visit to home; not verified (whether the restart played a part is not known).

## Where to find it

Home screen > the Chest Hunt card right of the Daily Challenge card (Daily Challenge, until then alone in the middle, moved left to make room). The card shows the time left in an orange tag, a chest, "Progress: 0/120" and a GO button [^s5]. After keys are collected the GO button carries a red dot [^s6]. GO opens the event panel (below), and the panel's PLAY opens the next regular level (level 11 here) [^s21] [^s22].

*The Chest Hunt card with its GO button, right of Daily Challenge*

## What it looks like

The event panel (it opened by itself under the start popup): a "CHEST HUNT" ribbon with an "i" button on the left and an X on the right, a large closed chest, "Progress: 0/120" over a bar with a key icon, the timer 2d 23h, the line "Collect all the keys and open the reward chest." and a green PLAY button [^s2].

![The Chest Hunt panel: the chest, Progress: 0/120, the key bar, 2d 23h, PLAY; i and X at the top](../img/20261006-chest-hunt-screen-e1354e16.webp) [^s2]
*The panel: progress toward 120 keys, the timer, PLAY; "i" top left, X top right*

Each time the panel opens, the claimed keys fly into the chest: a "+17" key badge appears at the top, keys move onto the chest, and the progress bar counts up from 0/120 to the total [^s21]. A frame taken during this count shows a lower number than the home card (15/120 against 17/120, and on a later visit 19/120 against 21/120); on the first visit the next frame showed the full 17/120 (the later visit had no second frame) [^s21] [^s23].

Then: "You need to put the letter E in this slot." with a hand on the E key [^s8]. E was typed: the letter went into the cell and the key icon over it was gone [^s9]. No clip: the span from the hand to the typed E is 24.5 s, and the typed-E step alone shows only the result.

On the later levels the key cells were outlined in white with the key tag on their top edge: three on the first screen of level 9, more on levels 10 and 11 (four on level 11), on the same boards as the lockers [^s10] [^s11] [^s22]. After a loss and Restart, the same quote came back with its keys on other cells [^s12].

### Claim keys

While the event runs the win card has no NEXT: under the speed banner (or the Quote Race strip, when that race runs) there are two buttons, a yellow "CLAIM" with a video icon and an "x3" badge, and an outlined plain "CLAIM" [^s4] [^s13]. The numbers per level: 12 / 4 on level 7, 15 / 5 on level 8, 9 / 3 on level 9, 15 / 5 on level 10, 12 / 4 on level 11 [^s4] [^s14] [^s13] [^s15] [^s24]. The free button gives the keys collected in the level (level 9 had three key cells and gave 3; level 11 had four, one of them filled by a [hint](hints.md), and gave 4: inferred, a key cell revealed by a hint counts) [^s25] [^s24]; inferred: the video button triples them. The free CLAIM was tapped every time; after it came an interstitial ad (a full-screen playable with no close button on levels 7 and 9) [^s16] [^s17].

## How it works

All in 3.6.1:

- Goal: 120 keys open the reward chest [^s2].
- Timer: 2d 23h at the start; 2d 21h on the card the next session, about two hours later; 2d 11h about twelve hours after the start [^s1] [^s19] [^s21].
- Keys: cells of the regular level marked with a key; the correct letter in the cell collects it [^s3].
- Claim on the win card: 3 to 5 keys free, or three times as many for a video (levels 7-11); the free claim equals the key cells filled in the level, a hinted cell included (inferred from level 11) [^s4] [^s15] [^s24].
- Pace: 0 → 4 → 9 → 12 → 17 → 21 keys over levels 7-11 with free claims; at that rate 120 keys take about 25-30 levels (inferred) [^s19] [^s26].
- A loss by 3 mistakes forfeits the keys collected in that level: the loss popup lists "key -2" next to "heart -1" after two key cells were filled on level 12; with no key collected (level 11, an earlier session) it lists only the heart [^s20] [^s27]. Whether the home card then drops by 2 was not read. Restart moves the key cells [^s12] [^s28].
- What the chest holds, what the "i" shows, and what happens at the timer's end were not seen.
