# Daily Challenge

A daily mode with a card on the home screen. On a fresh install of 3.6.1 it is locked behind completed levels; its content was not seen [^s1].

## Why it appeared

The card is on the home screen from the first launch, locked [^s1].

## Where to find it

Home screen > the Daily Challenge card at the top, under the lives counter and the gear. On a fresh install it shows a calendar marked LOCKED and "Complete 15 more levels to unlock"; a tap on the locked card does nothing [^s2].
