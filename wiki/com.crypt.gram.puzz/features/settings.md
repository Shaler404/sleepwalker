# Settings

A popup over the home screen with two toggles (Notifications, Vibration), four rows that open something (Restore Purchases, Support, Promo Code, How to play) and two links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Use) [^s1].

## Why it appeared

The gear at the top right of the home screen is there from the first launch [^s1].

## Where to find it

Home screen > the gear at the top right [^s1].

![The home screen; the gear that opens Settings is at the top right](../img/20261003-settings-entry-a5930d29.webp) [^s2]
*The gear at the top right of the home screen opens Settings*

## What it looks like

![The Settings popup over the home screen: eight rows from Notifications to Terms of Use and a blue close X at the top right](../img/20261003-settings-screen-c36b2c96.webp) [^s1]
*Settings: two toggles, four rows with icons, two underlined links, the blue X at the top right*

The popup is titled Settings and has a blue close X at its top right; the X closes it back to home [^s1] [^s3].
