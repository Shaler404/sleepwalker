# Tactics

## Decoding a quote
- Decode the whole quote before typing, from one sharp shot: given letters, one-letter words (A or
  I), repeated-letter shapes (TEST = 22 24 6 22) and recognising a famous quote. Level 9 ("A census
  taker once tried to test me…") was decoded in about 1 min [s:20261001-020937-chrono-2FYKPJ#8].
- Keyboard colours are a free check: green = the letter is placed somewhere, grey = the letter is
  complete everywhere [s:20261001-020937-chrono-2FYKPJ#14].
- Lock icons hide a cell's number until the cursor reaches it; typing straight through in cursor
  order opened all 4 locks of level 9 with 0 mistakes [s:20261001-020937-chrono-2FYKPJ#12] [s:20261001-020937-chrono-2FYKPJ#14].
- Keep a typing batch to one line or word group (5–13 taps): an interstitial can appear in the
  middle of a level and eats the rest of a long batch [s:20261001-020937-chrono-2FYKPJ#15].
- The quote can be longer than the screen (scroll bar on the right): look below before the last
  batch [s:20261001-020937-chrono-2FYKPJ#8].
