## pins: Pull the pins (draft)
- Goal: get coloured balls into the cup; grey balls must be painted first [s:20260930-192115-chrono-2FYKPJ#25] [s:20260930-201034-chrono-2FYKPJ#50].
- Controls: tap a pin's ring; swipes and taps on the rod do nothing [s:20260930-192115-chrono-2FYKPJ#4].
- Rules: coloured balls paint grey ones on contact; grey balls in the cup lose the level at once;
  bombs must not reach the cup (unverified what they do) [s:20260930-201034-chrono-2FYKPJ#50].
- Risks: every pin is irreversible: pull one at a time and look at the frame after each.
- Method: manual. Look at where every pin leads, plan the order, pause for painting.
- Level plan: pins above the colour first, the way to the cup last.
- Times (old format, rough): levels 1–8 took 25–65 s of play each; level 5 (4 stages) 2.7 min
  [s:20260930-192115-chrono-2FYKPJ#72] [s:20260930-201034-chrono-2FYKPJ#65].
