# Pull the Pin


[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.maroieqrwlk.unpin) · analysed on 241.5.1 (Google Play has 241.5.1)

A physics puzzle: pull pins so coloured balls paint the grey ones and fall into a cup. Level types:
normal, Multi Stage (3-4 boards in a row), hard (skull), colour bucket (two colours, two cups, from
level 20), a challenge offered at level 21 and boss levels (level 27: tall, 25 moves). Around the core:
a level map with keys and a key chest room, coins, a gift bar, a timed chest, collections (gacha,
puzzles, skins, weekly trophies), daily rewards, daily tasks (from level 14), leagues with golden pins,
a 6-hour level race, a win streak, extra modes from level 16, and banner, interstitial and rewarded ads.
