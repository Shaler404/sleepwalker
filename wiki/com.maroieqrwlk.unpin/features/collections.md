# Collections menu

> Recheck on v241.5.1: documented on 241.3.1; Google Play has 241.5.1.


The box with the coin counter right of Play! on the map opens Collections [s:20260930-192115-chrono-2FYKPJ#29] [s:20260930-201034-chrono-2FYKPJ#1]. Tabs:
[gacha](gacha.md), weekly trophies, [puzzles](puzzle-collection.md) and [skins](skins.md) (brush)
[s:20260930-201034-chrono-2FYKPJ#5]. A "Tap to continue" overlay on the map can eat the first tap [s:20260930-192115-chrono-2FYKPJ#29].

## Weekly trophies
"Collect trophies to win rewards", "Time left for this week 2d 6h" (at 2026-09-30 20:13), one chest
and 6 trophies with the first one checked, and a "Catch up on missed chests" line; tapping the chest
or the trophies does nothing [s:20260930-201034-chrono-2FYKPJ#5] [s:20260930-201034-chrono-2FYKPJ#7].
