# Bronze League leaderboard

The leaderboard screen that comes after a won level, titled **Bronze League**. It is the league of the Pull
Fest Ranking event, not a separate feature: the same Time Left timer, the same Pins score and the same rank
that the map's Bronze League banner shows [^s1] [^s2]. Everything about it is on the
[Pull Fest Ranking](pull-fest.md) page.

## Why it appeared

After the level 10 win, between the Puzzle Piece Found screen and the "Level completed!" screen; the same
name is on the banner at the top of the map [^s1] [^s2].

## Where to find it

The red **BRONZE LEAGUE** banner at the top of the map, with "Player" and the rank under it (868 at the
start of the session) [^s2]; and by itself after each won level [^s1].

![The map at level 10: the Bronze League banner at the top with Player and Rank 868](../img/20261003-ball-jar-entry-95e225ed.webp) [^s2]
*The map at level 10: the BRONZE LEAGUE banner at the top, Player and Rank 868 under it*

## What it looks like

![After the level 10 win: Bronze League, Time Left 6d 5h, Pins 27, the player at 847, Next Level!](../img/20261003-bronze-league-screen-bf36c194.webp) [^s1]
*After the level 10 win: Bronze League with two cups, Time Left 6d 5h, Pins 27, the player's row at 847, Next Level! (other names blacked out)*

A back arrow at the top left, the **BRONZE LEAGUE** ribbon between two bronze cups, "Time Left 6d 5h", the
**Pins** counter (27), the **LEADERBOARD** list with rank, flag and name, the player's row in yellow, and
**Next Level!** [^s1].

## How it works

See [Pull Fest Ranking](pull-fest.md): one point per pin pulled, a timer (6d 5h left on this screen), ranks near 1000.
After the level 10 win the player's row moved from 868 to 847 with 27 pins [^s1].
