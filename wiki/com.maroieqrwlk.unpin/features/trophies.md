# Weekly trophy chest

A weekly reward track on the second tab of [Collections](collections.md): a row of six trophies leads to a
chest, with a timer for the week. The tab's line reads "Collect trophies to win rewards" [^s1]. At level 10 the
first trophy was ticked; what earns a trophy and what the chest holds were not seen [^s1].

## Why it appeared

Hypothesis: present on the Collections trophies tab from the first Collections visit (L3+); the first trophy tier was already reached at L10, likely by level wins, not verified [^s1].
The tab already showed the first trophy ticked and 6d 5h left on the first visit to Collections, after level 9
[^s3].

## Where to find it

The map: the Collections box button right of **Play!**, then the orange trophy tab, the second of the four tabs
at the top of Collections [^s2].

![Collections: the orange trophy tab, second of the four tabs](../img/20261003-trophies-entry-e1974a37.webp) [^s2]
*Collections opens on the gumball tab; the orange trophy tab is the second icon tab, right of the blue gumball tab*

## What it looks like

![Trophies tab: weekly chest with 6d 5h left, six trophy tiers (first ticked), 'Catch up on missed chests'](../img/20261003-trophies-screen-f4371f7a.webp) [^s1]
*The trophies tab: "Time left for this week" 6d 5h, the golden chest, six trophies (the first ticked), "Catch up on missed chests"*

From top to bottom [^s1]:

- "Collect trophies to win rewards";
- "Time left for this week" with a timer (6d 5h);
- a golden chest;
- a row of six trophies: the first, red, with a tick; five dark;
- "Catch up on missed chests".

## How it works

Version 241.5.1.

- Six trophies per week, a chest at the end; one ticked at level 10 [^s1].
- The timer read 6d 5h both on the first Collections visit (about 21:00) and in the next session (21:40)
  [^s3] [^s1]; it shows days and hours only.
- Inferred: the six trophies match the six [Pull Fest](pull-fest.md) league cups, and the first was ticked when
  the Pull Fest Bronze League began; not verified.
- "Catch up on missed chests": inferred to offer chests of past weeks; not tapped.
