# Remove Ads

A button on the level screen marked with the word ADS crossed out by a red prohibition sign [^s1]. The
game shows banner ads at the bottom of the level, win and Settings screens from level 1 [^s2] [^s1]. What
the button offers was not opened this session.

## Why it appeared

Always on the level screen, ADS button top right [^s1].

## Where to find it

The level screen: the square ADS button under the restart button, top right; it is there from level 1
[^s1] [^s3].
