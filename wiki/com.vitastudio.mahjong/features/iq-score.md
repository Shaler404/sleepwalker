# IQ score

A number labelled "IQ" at the top of every level screen: 40 at level 19 [^s1]. What raises it was not
seen in this session.

## Why it appeared

On the level screen when level 19 was opened [^s1]. Earlier sessions saw tiles marked "IQ+N" on the
board; that is from the map, not seen in this session [^s1].

## Where to find it

<!-- no-entry: a HUD counter; it has no control of its own, it is on the level screen (see Tray mahjong level) -->
On the level screen, top centre, between the back arrow and the menu button [^s1].

![The level 19 screen: "IQ: 40" in gold and white at the top centre, above the 4-slot tray](../img/20261003-iq-score-screen-d06a7bf0.webp) [^s1]

Version 3.40.1. 40 at level 19, before any tile was played [^s1]. What earns IQ, whether it carries
between levels and whether it unlocks anything are not verified.
