# Leagues


Unlocked by the level-10 win: the next Level tap shows a Bronze League popup, then matchmaking for a
group of 50 ("2/49") [s:20260930-235817-chrono-2FYKPJ#43] [s:20260930-235817-chrono-2FYKPJ#44]. The rules (info, two pages): collect Elite Tiles, reach the
Top 10 to be promoted, rewards when the timer ends; Glory league uses stars (promotion +5/+3/+2/+1,
demotion −2) with tiers Glory 0–9, Exalted 10–29, Supreme 30–49, Eternal 50–99, Legendary 100+
[s:20260930-235817-chrono-2FYKPJ#65] [s:20261001-010125-chrono-2FYKPJ#4] [s:20261001-010125-chrono-2FYKPJ#5].
