# Loading screen

A dark green screen shown while the game loads: a tagline, a progress bar and two counters of how many
people play [^s1].

## Why it appeared

Every launch, after the consent on the first one [^s1].

## Where to find it

<!-- no-entry: no control opens it; it shows on every launch -->
It shows by itself when the game starts [^s1].

## What it looks like

![The loading screen: a short tagline in quotes credited to Vita Mahjong at the top, a green progress bar with a mahjong tile riding on it, and two counters at the bottom: 212 million Games Today and 542 k Playing Now](../img/20261003-loading-screen-c0f17e2f.webp) [^s1]
*Tagline, progress bar, and the Games Today and Playing Now counters*

- A tagline in quotes, credited to Vita Mahjong: a claim that playing 30 minutes a day helps sleep
  (about a dozen words) [^s1].
- A green progress bar with a tile riding on its end [^s1].
- Two counters in laurel wreaths: "212 million Games Today" and "542 k Playing Now" [^s1].

## How it works

Version 3.40.1. No buttons. The counters' source is not known: whether they are live figures is not
verified [^s1].
