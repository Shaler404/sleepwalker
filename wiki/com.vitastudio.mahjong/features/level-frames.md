# Level avatar frames

Frames for the player's avatar, listed in Profile under a "Level" heading. At level 19 the list has one
frame, badged 10 [^s1].

## Why it appeared

Hypothesis: a frame is earned at level milestones, one per 10 levels (the one owned at level 19 is badged
10); not verified [^s1].

## Where to find it

Home screen > avatar (top left) > Profile > the "Frame" tab, right of "Avatar" [^s2].

![The Profile popup on its Avatar tab, with the Frame tab to the right of Avatar under the player name (blacked out)](../img/20261003-profile-tab-avatar-94ce6be0.webp) [^s2]
*The "Frame" tab, right of "Avatar" in Profile*

## What it looks like

![Profile, Frame tab: under "Level" one square frame with a blue ribbon badge reading 10; under it an empty "Default" heading; country (blacked out), PHOTO and Save below](../img/20261003-profile-tab-frame-c4ce6af1.webp) [^s1]
*The Frame tab at level 19: one Level frame badged 10, nothing under Default*

Two headings: "Level" with one blue-grey frame topped by a ribbon reading 10, and "Default" with nothing
under it [^s1]. Locked frames are not listed [^s1].

## How it works

Version 3.40.1. One frame at level 19 [^s1]. Whether it was selected, how a frame is put on and whether a
new one comes at level 20 or 30 are not verified (Save was not tapped).
