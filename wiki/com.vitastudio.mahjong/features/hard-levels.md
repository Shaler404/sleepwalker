# Hard levels


Level 10 is the first Hard level: the L9 win screen shows a red "Level 10 / Hard" button and the level
opens with a red flame banner [s:20260930-235817-chrono-2FYKPJ#21] [s:20260930-235817-chrono-2FYKPJ#22]. A Hard win shows "Genius!" ("This HARD level was no
match for your skills.") and an "x2" Elite tag on the next button [s:20260930-235817-chrono-2FYKPJ#41].
