# FTUE / tutorial levels


## How it works
- Level 1 is a tutorial in 3 steps: "Match identical tiles to clear them from the board", then "Remove
  edge tiles to unlock the trapped tiles", then a last pair; then the full board [s:20260930-203959-chrono-2FYKPJ#19] [s:20260930-203959-chrono-2FYKPJ#22] [s:20260930-203959-chrono-2FYKPJ#26].
- After a relaunch a half-played level 1 started again from the tutorial [s:20260930-211039-chrono-2FYKPJ#2].
- "Level N+1" on a win screen starts the next level at once [s:20260930-211039-chrono-2FYKPJ#118].
- Unlocks: booster counters from level 2 (Hint 5, Undo 10), Shuffle at level 6, the level-10 chest,
  leagues after the level-10 win, face-down tiles on level 11 [s:20260930-211039-chrono-2FYKPJ#118] [s:20260930-221457-chrono-2FYKPJ#78] [s:20260930-235817-chrono-2FYKPJ#42] [s:20260930-235817-chrono-2FYKPJ#43] [s:20260930-235817-chrono-2FYKPJ#51].
- No new main-screen sections through level 6 [s:20260930-221457-chrono-2FYKPJ#93].
