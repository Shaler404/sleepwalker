# Vita Mahjong


[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vitastudio.mahjong) · version 3.39.1

Tray mahjong: tap free tiles into a 4-slot tray; two identical tiles in the tray vanish. Around the
core: an IQ bar and combo tiers, Shuffle / Hint / Undo boosters, revives, a level chest every 10
levels, Hard levels, leagues with Elite tiles from level 10, Daily Victories, face-down tiles,
achievements, themes, rewarded ads and a No Ads purchase. No shop or currency through level 19.

## Coverage
- 14 sessions from a fresh install; level 18 won, level 19 in progress (v3.39.1). Surveys 1–3 found no
  new entry points; discovery is open (genre checklist gaps: shop and offers, interstitials, timed
  events and social features, language).
- The level mechanic is slow: typically 14 minutes a level on L14–L18 against a 5-minute budget. The
  local screenshot solver stalled on L18 and L19 and is not published; levels are played by hand
  ([how to play](agent/playbook.md)). Only L10 has been a Hard level so far.
- Waiting: Daily Victories day 4 (from 2026-10-03) and day 7 (from 2026-10-06), the 10-day chest (from
  2026-10-10), the league event end, and the level-20 chest; a fresh install is needed to see how
  Daily Victories starts.
