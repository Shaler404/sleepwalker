# Vita Mahjong


[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vitastudio.mahjong) · version 3.39.1

Tray mahjong: tap free tiles into a 4-slot tray; two identical tiles in the tray vanish. Around the
core: an IQ bar and combo tiers, Shuffle / Hint / Undo boosters, revives, a level chest every 10
levels, Hard levels, leagues with Elite tiles from level 10, Daily Victories, face-down tiles,
achievements, themes, rewarded ads and a No Ads purchase. No shop or currency through level 13.

## Coverage
- 8 sessions from a fresh install to level 13; surveys 1 and 2 found no new entry points; discovery is
  open (genre checklist gaps: shop and offers, interstitials, language).
- The level mechanic is slow: 6–30 minutes a level against a 5-minute budget, no solver yet.
