  ones), several in one batch, then the arrows they freed. Levels 1-3 were won Flawless this way in
  11-25 s of in-game time [s:20261001-204000-chrono-2FYKPJ#4] [s:20261001-204000-chrono-2FYKPJ#8] [s:20261001-204000-chrono-2FYKPJ#22].
