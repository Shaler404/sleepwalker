# Tactics

Full method per mechanic: [How to play](playbook.md).

- Swap a colour bomb with the order colour as soon as they touch: level 1 done in 6/28 moves, level 2
  in 6/25 [s:20261001-202316-chrono-2FYKPJ#11] [s:20261001-202316-chrono-2FYKPJ#18].
- On jelly, use the bomb on the colour that sits on the most remaining jelly cells: level 6 (58 jelly)
  won in about 8 of 25 moves [s:20261001-232021-chrono-2FYKPJ#52].
- Fish or striped plus a same-colour wrapped candy fire together and clear the bottom
  [s:20261001-202316-chrono-2FYKPJ#27].
- Against meringue, match on the rows and columns touching it, from the top edge down
  [s:20261001-202316-chrono-2FYKPJ#13-17].
- A swap of a special with a plain candy that makes no line is rejected and costs only time
  [s:20261001-232021-chrono-2FYKPJ#32].
