# Candy Crush Saga

[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.king.candycrushsaga) · version 1.335.1.2
on the phone (1.337.0.2 on Google Play)

King's classic match-3: swap adjacent candies to fill each level's order (collect a colour, clear
meringue, clear jelly) within a move limit. A fresh install opens on a Terms of Use popup, then a title
screen with Play, Retrieve My Progress (King account) and Settings. The first levels run on an FTUE
story path in a night forest (chapters: reach the bush, follow Dachs) instead of the saga map; through
level 7 the map, lives, shop and events have not appeared yet.

![Title screen: Play, Retrieve My Progress, settings gear bottom left](img/20261001-title-screen-screen-c7973a0b.webp)
