# Settings

Settings is a full-screen window where the player controls the game's music and sound effects and
reaches further menus for audio, game features, and account and help. It has two tabs: General and
Accessibility [^s1].

## Where to find it

From the title screen: the round pink gear in the bottom-left corner [^s1]. A gear of the same look also
sits at the left end of the booster bar under the board during a level [^s2]; it was not tapped, so
whether it opens this same window is not verified.

![Title screen: settings gear in the bottom-left corner](../img/20261001-settings-entry-c7b73a0b.webp) [^s3]

## What it looks like

A pink window titled "Settings" with a red close button (a cross) at the top right and two tabs under the
title: General (open by default) and Accessibility [^s1].

![Settings window, General tab open; Accessibility tab next to it, close button top right](../img/20261001-settings-screen-ea6a6442.webp) [^s1]

## What you can do

| Tab or button | What it does |
|---|---|
| [General](#general) | Music and Sound effects on/off and volume; links to More Audio, Features, Account & Help |
| [Accessibility](#accessibility) | Second tab (not opened) |

### General

The tab that opens first. Two cards with a toggle and a volume slider each: Music and Sound effects. On a
fresh install both toggles are on and both sliders are at maximum. Below them, three rows with a red arrow
button each: More Audio, Features, and Account & Help; each presumably opens its own sub-menu (inferred,
none was opened) [^s1].

![Settings with General and Accessibility tabs: Music and Sound effects toggles with sliders, More Audio, Features, Account & Help](../img/20261001-settings-tab-general-ea6a6442.webp) [^s1]

### Accessibility

The second tab, next to General. It was not opened in this session.

## How it works

- Defaults on a fresh install (version 1.335.1.2): Music on, Sound effects on, both at full volume
  [^s1].
- The close cross returns to the screen Settings was opened from (the title screen here) [^s4].
