# Mailbox icon

An envelope icon at the left end of the map's top bar. In this session it opened nothing: two taps left the
screen unchanged [^s3] [^s4]. Inferred from the icon: an inbox for messages or gifts; not verified.

## Why it appeared

The envelope is in the top bar from the first view of the map, after level 1 [^s2]. Hypothesis: the tap does
nothing while the inbox is empty or the game is not tied to an account, not verified [^s1].

## Where to find it

The envelope at the left end of the top bar, left of the lives heart; it is there on the map and on the
Events, Social and Shop tabs [^s2].

![The level map; the envelope is at the left end of the top bar, left of the heart with 5](../img/20261003-mailbox-entry-9af4f0af.webp) [^s2]
*Envelope at the left end of the top bar of the map*

## What it looks like

<!-- no-screen: two taps on the envelope changed nothing on screen (steps 15 and 22) -->
No screen opened. The envelope is pale, with a small heart on its flap, and showed no badge or counter
[^s2].

## How it works

Version 1.337.0.2. Both taps were made with the Shop tab open; the frame after each was the same as before
[^s3] [^s4].
