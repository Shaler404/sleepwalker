# Settings


Gear at the top right of Home and of every level (670,110) [s:20261001-013526-chrono-2FYKPJ#82].

![Home settings: music, sound, voice and vibration toggles, Save your progress, Language, Feedback, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, version 1.18.0](../img/20261001-home-settings-d0163f85.webp)

## How it works
- Home popup: music, sound, voice, vibration; Save your progress; Language; Feedback; Terms of
  Service; Privacy Policy; version 1.18.0 [s:20261001-022624-chrono-2FYKPJ#67].
- In-level popup: the four toggles, Pattern Mode, Feedback, Restart [s:20261001-013526-chrono-2FYKPJ#82].
- Music is OFF by default with a red dot that goes after the first toggle [s:20261001-022624-chrono-2FYKPJ#30].
- Pattern Mode ON puts an icon on every colour region (stars, bells, paws, ...); the solver still
  reads the board [s:20261001-013526-chrono-2FYKPJ#85] [s:20261001-022624-chrono-2FYKPJ#35].
- Restart: an interstitial (video with "Next"), then a reset board, 3 fish and score 0; spent boosters
  are not refunded [s:20261001-013526-chrono-2FYKPJ#84] [s:20261001-013526-chrono-2FYKPJ#85].
