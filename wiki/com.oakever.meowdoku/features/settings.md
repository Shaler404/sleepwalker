# Settings

A popup over Home with four audio and vibration toggles, a "Save your progress" row, the Language and
Feedback buttons, links to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy, and the version number [^s1].

## Why it appeared

Gear icon on Home, top right, red dot [^s1].

## Where to find it

On [Home](home.md), the gear icon top right (it carries a red dot) opens Settings [^s1]. A gear with a red dot
is also on the level screen, top right [^s3]; it was not opened there.

![Home screen: the gear icon top right (with a red dot) opens Settings](../img/20261003-settings-entry-ad85780f.webp) [^s2]
*Home screen: the gear icon top right (with a red dot) opens Settings*

## What it looks like

![Music/sound/voice/vibration toggles, Save your progress, Language, Feedback, ToS, Privacy, v1.19.1](../img/20261003-settings-screen-90663f85.webp) [^s1]
*Settings: four toggles (music off with a red dot), Save your progress, Language, Feedback, the two links and the version*

What is on the popup, top to bottom [^s1]:

- Four toggles: music (a crossed-out note) OFF with a red dot; sound (a speaker) ON; voice (a speaking person) ON;
  vibration (a vibrating phone) ON.
- "Save your progress", a row with an arrow.
- Two buttons: Language, Feedback.
- Two links: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy.
- The cross top right closes the popup and returns to Home [^s4].

None of the toggles, rows, buttons or links was tapped in this session.

The popup shows "Version 1.19.1" at the bottom [^s1].

## How it works

- Music was off on a fresh install and its toggle carried the red dot also seen on the gear on Home [^s1].
  Inferred: the red dot on the gear points at the music toggle.
- Nothing else was changed in this session.

Version 1.19.1.
