# Profile (name, avatar, frame, skins)

A popup where the player sets how they appear: the name, the avatar picture, the avatar's frame, and the
skin of the cat placed on the board. The avatar and frame chosen here are the ones shown on Home and on the
[Fish leaderboard](fish-leaderboard.md) [^s1].

## Why it appeared

Avatar button top-left of Home [^s1].

## Where to find it

On [Home](home.md), the avatar button top left opens the Profile popup [^s1].

![Home screen: the avatar button top left opens Profile](../img/20261003-profile-entry-ad85780f.webp) [^s2]
*Home screen: the avatar button top left opens Profile*

## What it looks like

![Profile popup: avatar, player name with pencil, tabs Skins/Avatar/Frame, Confirm](../img/20261003-profile-screen-84977b68.webp) [^s1]
*Profile: the current avatar in its frame, the name field with a pencil (blacked out: the account's name), the tabs Skins, Avatar, Frame, and Confirm*

At the top: the current avatar in its frame, and the player's name in a field with a pencil button. The
default name is a six-character code [^s1]. Below: three tabs and the orange Confirm button; a cross closes
the popup [^s5]. It opens on the Avatar tab [^s1].

## What you can do

| Tab or button | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Skins](#skins) | the cat skins: 1 owned, 7 locked |
| [Avatar](#avatar) | 8 avatar pictures, all selectable |
| [Frame](#frame) | avatar frames in sets; the Classic set in colours |

The pencil next to the name (to rename) and Confirm (to apply a choice) were not tapped.

### Skins

![1 cat skin owned, 7 silhouettes locked](../img/20261003-profile-tab-skins-94966b69.webp) [^s3]
*Skins tab: the black-and-white cat with a check mark, seven greyed silhouettes*

Eight slots: the black-and-white cat used on the board, owned and selected (check mark), and seven cat
silhouettes that are locked [^s3]. Tapping a locked silhouette showed nothing: no price, no condition
[^s6]. How they unlock is on [Cat skins](cat-skins.md).

### Avatar

![Profile: name, tabs Skins/Avatar/Frame, Confirm](../img/20261003-profile-tab-avatar-84977b68.webp) [^s1]
*Avatar tab: eight animal pictures, the corgi selected*

Eight pictures in two rows: a black-and-white cat, a panda, a corgi (selected), a crocodile, a chicken, a duck
in a cap, a lion, a calico cat [^s1]. None is locked [^s1].

### Frame

![Classic frames, more frame sets above (scrolls)](../img/20261003-profile-tab-frame-c1863e39.webp) [^s4]
*Frame tab: decorated frames at the top, the Classic set below with the red frame selected*

A scrolling list of frame sets. Above the "Classic" heading, a row of decorated frames (paws, bells, bows);
the Classic set holds plain frames in eight colours, the red one selected (the frame around the avatar on
Home) [^s4]. Whether the decorated frames are owned was not checked. The [Fish leaderboard](fish-leaderboard.md)
rules name "exclusive frames" as a reward [^s7].

## How it works

- Choice of avatar and frame: all eight avatars and the Classic frames are open [^s1] [^s4].
- Skins: one of eight owned on this account [^s3].
- Nothing was changed or confirmed in this session.

Version 1.19.1.
