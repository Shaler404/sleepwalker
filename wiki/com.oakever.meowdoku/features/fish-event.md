# Fish leaderboard event


## How it works
- After the level 10 win a "New Session" popup with a 24h timer: "Play games to collect fish and rank
  up during each event" [s:20260930-233055-chrono-2FYKPJ#49].
- From level 11 every win shows the leaderboard first ("You rose by N places", hearts from other
  players, Tap to Continue) [s:20260930-233055-chrono-2FYKPJ#53] [s:20261001-022624-chrono-2FYKPJ#16].
- The podium with a timer on Home opens the leaderboard: ranks 1–3 have gift boxes; an (i) page says
  "Clear main levels → Top the leaderboard → Win exclusive frames and rewards"; "Go to Collect" starts
  the next level [s:20261001-022624-chrono-2FYKPJ#8] [s:20261001-022624-chrono-2FYKPJ#9] [s:20261001-022624-chrono-2FYKPJ#11].
- Fish earned seem to be the fish left after a flawless level (3 per win) [s:20260930-233055-chrono-2FYKPJ#53].
