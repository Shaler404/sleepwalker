# Settings


Gear at the top right of Home and of every level (665,127) [s:20261001-003641-chrono-2FYKPJ#17] [s:20261001-024647-chrono-2FYKPJ#58].

## How it works
- Popup: sound and vibration toggles (grey with a slash when off, green when on), Help Center,
  Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, "Version 1.0.2 (Build 190)" and the User ID [s:20261001-003641-chrono-2FYKPJ#17] [s:20261001-035425-chrono-2FYKPJ#17].
- In a level the popup adds a green Restart at the spot where Home has Help Center (364,1225): it plays
  an interstitial, then a clean board with 3 hearts [s:20261001-035425-chrono-2FYKPJ#42] [s:20261001-035425-chrono-2FYKPJ#43].
