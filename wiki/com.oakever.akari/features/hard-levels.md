# Hard levels


Every 10th level is Hard: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50. The previous win screen shows a red "Hard" tag on the
next-level button and the level a "Hard" label under the title; same rules and 3 hearts, no extra
reward [s:20261001-003641-chrono-2FYKPJ#21] [s:20261001-003641-chrono-2FYKPJ#22] [s:20261001-024647-chrono-2FYKPJ#80] [s:20261001-035425-chrono-2FYKPJ#31].
