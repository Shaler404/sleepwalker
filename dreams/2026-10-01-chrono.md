## Solvers

Published (both mechanics won all their levels well within the 5-minute budget):
- `solvers/com.oakever.akari/akari.py` — backtracking Light Up on a hand-read board. Only computes. The
  dream added a check that rejects unknown cell characters and documented the `count` key. Known limit:
  it takes the first solution without checking uniqueness.
- `solvers/com.oakever.meowdoku/queens.py` — reads the board from the screenshot, clusters colours,
  backtracks. Only computes. The critic found its plausibility check could never fire, so the dream
  changed it: no moves for a grid outside 4–12, a colour region that is not connected, more cats than
  rows, or a board with more than one solution; `done` only when N non-attacking cats are on the board;
  moves are now `[x, y, 2]` double taps (3 per round with `rescan`). Tested against the original on
  261 frames of the three sessions: the same moves on all 198 board frames; it now refuses 63 frames
  where the original read popups, ads, the intro animation or the win animation as boards. The local
  copy in `state/` differs until this is merged.
